FMCG Excess Inventory Risk Prediction

Decision-Tree-Based Excess Inventory Risk Prediction for FMCG Inventory Management

Inventory→Data→Machine Learning→Risk Prediction→Business Decision

Problem

Excess inventory increases holding costs and ties up working capital.

Approach

Supervised binary classification using a Decision Tree.

Input

Inventory, sales, demand, replenishment, reorder, lead time, promotion and seasonality features.

Output

7-day excess inventory risk prediction. Target: Excess_Inventory_Risk_7D (1 = risk, 0 = no risk).

Business Value

Helps inventory teams identify potentially risky products earlier and support better inventory decisions.

Includes

Dataset, data analysis, ML model, risk prediction, product risk status, dashboard concept, GitHub repository.
